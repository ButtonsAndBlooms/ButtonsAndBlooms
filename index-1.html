Our Story

Welcome to Buttons & Blooms! What started out as a simple kitchen-table hobby has grown into a small oasis of handcrafted art. We love collecting lost elements like vintage buttons and combining them with nature's briefest masterpieces—pressed flowers. Every single item we create is unique, made with care, and shipped out in recycled eco-friendly packaging.
